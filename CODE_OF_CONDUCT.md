# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want a respectful, constructive community for developers, security researchers, auditors, and privacy advocates. Contributions may concern sensitive systems and incidents; that is never a reason to disregard the people involved.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and evidence in good faith.
- Respect confidentiality and coordinated disclosure.
- Give feedback about code, policies, or designs—not personal traits.
- Be especially careful not to share private keys, confidential source code, user data, or unapproved security reports.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Doxxing or publication of confidential reports.
- Posting credentials, private traces, customer data, or exploit details before coordinated disclosure.
- Misrepresenting a tool finding as an audit, certification, or confirmed exploit.

## Enforcement

Before public launch, maintainers must publish a real conduct-reporting contact and enforcement process. Do not make this repository public until that contact replaces this placeholder policy.
